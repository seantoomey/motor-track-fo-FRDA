Weekly Testing Guidelines

Please follow these guidelines every week to help keep the research results consistent.

  • Use the same device for every weekly test.
  • Keep your screen orientation the same.
  • Complete the tests in a quiet environment.
  • Follow the same testing instructions each week.
  • Try to complete your test at approximately the same time each week.
  • Avoid switching devices during the study. If you need to change devices, let the researcher know.

Your results will be securely recorded for research purposes. You will not be shown previous scores or asked to beat them.
