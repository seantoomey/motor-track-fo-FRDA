Understanding motor function over time.

MotorMonitor is a research project exploring how simple, repeated motor tests can help us understand changes in motor performance, with a focus on Friedreich's ataxia.

The purpose

Why monitor motor performance?

Motor abilities can involve coordination, reaction time, accuracy and controlled movement. Studying these areas repeatedly may help researchers understand patterns that are not obvious from a single test.

MotorMonitor focuses on weekly observations rather than comparing participants against one another.

The activities

Four motor tests

Each activity explores a different part of motor performance.

✦

Target Tapping

Measures how accurately and consistently a participant can tap a target.

◎

Moving Target

Explores visual tracking and the ability to follow a moving target.

◷

Reaction Time

Measures how quickly a participant responds to a visual signal.

⇄

Alternating Taps

Explores coordination and the ability to alternate between two targets.

The science

Friedreich's ataxia research

Friedreich's ataxia is a genetic condition that can affect coordination and movement. MotorMonitor explores whether simple digital activities could provide useful observations of motor performance over time. The project is educational research and is not a diagnostic tool.

Explore the Research
About the project

Built as a Young Scientist project

MotorMonitor was developed to investigate how accessible digital technology could be used to study motor performance. It combines website development, interactive tests and research into Friedreich's ataxia.

The aim is to explore the potential of repeated observations while recognising that a school research project cannot replace clinical assessment or medical research.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How often should I complete the tests?

The project is designed around weekly testing sessions, following the researcher's instructions.

Should I use the same device each time?

Yes. Using the same device and keeping conditions consistent can help make weekly observations more comparable.

Can I see my previous scores?

Previous scores are not displayed to participants. This helps avoid pressure to improve or beat an earlier result.

Is MotorMonitor a medical test?

No. MotorMonitor is a school research prototype and cannot diagnose, monitor or treat a medical condition.

What is Friedreich's ataxia?

Friedreich's ataxia is an inherited condition associated with changes in the FXN gene. It can affect the nervous system and coordination.

Important research notice: MotorMonitor is an educational research prototype. It is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis, treatment or clinical advice. Participation should follow the researcher's approved study instructions.
