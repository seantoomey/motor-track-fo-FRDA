FRIEDREICH'S ATAXIA RESEARCH PROJECT

One minute every week.
Better safe than sorry.

MotorTrack is a one-minute weekly digital motor test developed as part of the Stripe Young Scientist & Technology Exhibition investigating the link between motor performance and Friedreich's ataxia (FRDA). -created by Sean Toomey-

Start Weekly Test

How MotorTrack works

01

Take the test

Complete a series of short motor and reaction tests lasting around one minute.

02

Build your baseline

Your results are recorded over time to understand your normal level of performance.

03

Track changes

Future results can be compared with your previous performance to identify persistent changes that wouldn't be normal factoring in aging and other natural causes of worsening motor control.

What's measured?

🎯

Target Tapping

Coordination and accuracy

✋

Moving Target

Motor control and tracking

⚡

Reaction Time

Speed of response

🔄

Alternating Taps

Fine motor control and consistency

Important

MotorTrack is a research prototype and is not a medical diagnostic tool. Changes in test performance can have many possible causes and should not be interpreted as a diagnosis of Friedreich's ataxia.
